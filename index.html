The game

Everything a robot commander needs

Start with a patch of scrap and a few rusty bots. Turn it into an island base and an army that can take on the world.

Build your island

Place mines, factories, power plants and hangars. Upgrade them, unlock new islands and visit other players' bases.

Collect and upgrade

Build robots on land, sea and air, fit them with weapons, level them up and star them up. Mix squads that fit your style.

Battle the world

Turn-based fights where timing counts: fire volleys, guard, swap robots. Challenge friends or climb the ranked ladder.

Campaign

50 missions across 5 worlds

From dusty wastelands to the open sky, every world brings new enemies and new battlefields.

Ranked PvP

Prove who has the best squad

Take your squad online and face players from around the world. Win to climb the leaderboard, or challenge a friend with their player ID.

  • Live turn-based battles
  • Ranked ladder with a worldwide leaderboard
  • Friendly challenges with a player ID
  • Preset emotes, no chat, so the game stays friendly
