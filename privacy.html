Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what the game "Botland" (package com.botland.game, "the game") collects and why. Botland is made by an independent developer, contact: shahfahadiffi@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account identifier. When you start the game it signs you in anonymously through Google Firebase Authentication, which creates a random player ID. If you choose to sign in with Google, we also receive the account identifier Google provides for that sign-in.
  • Game data. Your player name, level, currencies, islands, buildings, robots, weapons, battle results and settings, stored on our server so your progress is kept and battles work.
  • Technical logs. Each request to our server is logged with time, request address, response status and your player ID, and your IP address. Logs are used to fix problems and prevent abuse and are deleted after 14 days.
  • Battle emotes. Preset emotes only. The game has no free-text or voice chat.

Ads and purchases

The game may show optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device information to serve and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings. If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your real name, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Who we share data with

Only service providers needed to run the game: Google (Firebase sign-in, AdMob, Google Play) and our hosting provider (OVHcloud, servers in Singapore). We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Children

Botland is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

We keep your game data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete it at any time, see Delete your data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored on a protected server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
