Botlands account and data deletion

This page explains how to delete your Botlands account (package com.botland.game) and the data linked to it. Botlands is made by an independent developer.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to shahfahadiffi@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Botlands data.
  2. In the email, include your player name and level, and your player ID if you can (open your profile in the game). This lets us find the right account.
  3. We delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player name, country and level
  • Your progress: islands, buildings, robots, weapons, currencies and settings
  • Your battle history and leaderboard entry
  • The link to your Google account, if you signed in with Google

What is kept

Technical server logs (request time, status, player ID and IP address) are not removed on request. They are deleted automatically after 14 days. Anything we are legally required to keep is kept only as long as the law says.

Deleting the game from your phone does not delete your data on our server. Deleting the account is permanent: progress and purchases of in-game items cannot be restored afterwards.

Questions? shahfahadiffi@gmail.com
